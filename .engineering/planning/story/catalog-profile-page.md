## Story

`rfcspec-docs generate` writes one page per profile, `website/docs/catalog/<name>.md`, served at
`/rfcspec/docs/catalog/<name>`: its RFCs, its requirement matrix and the ESS gaps it hit.

## Acceptance

`task check` passes with a generated `website/docs/catalog/time.md` that links RFC 868 to
rfc-editor.org, lists all 6 requirements of `profiles/time/overlay.yaml` with their statuses and
says no ESS gap was hit, and fails when that page is stale, edited or orphaned.

- RFCs: one row per RFC of `profile.yaml`, number linking to
  `https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc<number>`, title, sections in scope, kind from the overlay.
- Requirement matrix: one row per overlay requirement, in overlay order: id, level, transports
  (`all` when empty), quote, source span, status, and the satisfying element or the reason. A
  unit test fails if the row count differs from the overlay requirement count, for `profiles/time`
  and for the fixture profile of `story:catalog-index`.
- ESS gaps: every `unsupported` status whose reason names `ess-gap G<n>` (design section 7),
  grouped by gap id, each with the requirements it stops and a link to `/docs/design` (the gaps
  table). `profiles/time` shows the sentence "No ESS gap: every requirement in scope lowers."; the
  fixture shows G1 with its one requirement. An `unsupported` reason that names no gap is listed
  under "Unsupported, no ESS gap" and never dropped.
- The index row of each profile links to its page, and `task website` builds with no broken
  link under `/docs/catalog/` (Docusaurus `onBrokenLinks` as configured).
- Orphan rule of `story:catalog-index` holds for profile pages: a `website/docs/catalog/<name>.md`
  without a `profiles/<name>/` fails `generate --check` and is deleted by `generate`.
- Quotes and titles pass through `page::cell`; `generate --check` passive check stays green; a
  unit test renders a quote holding `{`, `<Tag>` and `|` and asserts the escaped cell.
- No communication semantics section: `profiles/time/profile.yaml` declares none and how a
  TCP-and-UDP profile would is open (`decision-blocker:catalog-multi-transport-semantics`). No
  conformance verdict (`decision-blocker:catalog-conformance-verdict`).

## Domain relations

- Profile -> Rfc, one-to-many, references only. Inferable (inferred from
  `ess/domains/profile.yaml`, type `rfcspec.profile.ProfileRfc`, a field typed
  `rfcspec.catalogue.RfcNumber` with no `relations:` entry, and `crates/rfcspec-model/src/lib.rs:29`).
- Profile -> Status, one-to-many, owned: `ess/domains/profile.yaml`, entity
  `rfcspec.profile.Profile`, relation `statuses`.
- Status -> Requirement, one: `ess/domains/profile.yaml`, entity `rfcspec.profile.Status`,
  relation `requirement`.
- Status (unsupported) -> ESS gap, zero or one gap per status, the gap is no entity. Inferable
  (inferred from `ess/domains/profile.yaml`, variant `rfcspec.profile.StatusKind.Unsupported`,
  summary "with a reason, such as an ESS gap id", and design section 7, "marks the requirement
  status `unsupported: ess-gap G1`"; no `relations:` entry declares it).

## Scope

`crates/rfcspec-docs` (`catalog.rs`, its tests), `website/docs/catalog/time.md` (generated).
Reads `profiles/*/profile.yaml` and `overlay.yaml`, writes nothing under `profiles/`.

Depends on `story:catalog-index`: it extends the same `crates/rfcspec-docs/src/catalog.rs`, the
outputs map and orphan rule that story introduces in `main.rs`, reuses its fixture profile and
`Overlay.kind`, and relies on its autogenerated sidebar category, so it needs no
`website/sidebars.ts` edit.
