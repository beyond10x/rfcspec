Findings, one line each (paths under `~/beyond10x/rfcspec/.engineering/planning/story/`):

- `story:dns-query-machine` — its overlay-model work (participant `fields`, `set`, guards, `local`/`input` operands) is the same outcome as `story:websocket-machine-guards`, and no edge orders them. Add `depends_on: story:websocket-machine-guards`, and cut this story's model work to the `state_implication` property and its check. Otherwise the second to land redefines or conflicts with the first in `rfcspec-model`, `check.rs` and `rfcspec-ess`. The "no guards" premise at dns-query-machine.md:49 is already false once the frames epic lands. — `dns-query-machine.md:49`, `dns-query-machine.md:69`, `websocket-machine-guards.md:23-33`, `websocket-machine-guards.md:36`, `aep plan artifact graph`
- `story:websocket-proposal-slice` — it shares the websocket page pins and `profile.yaml` sections with `story:websocket-ping-machine` and `story:websocket-fragments-machine`, and no edge orders the two epics.
  - **Shared pins:** "Reviewed" is `overlay.requirements.len()` (`catalog.rs:532-535` in the worktree). Accepted or edited proposals therefore change the "Reviewed: 57", "reviewed stays 57", "Reviewed: 70 … 126 unreviewed" and index-row pins.
  - **Sections:** this story asserts the sections stay "(5.2, 5.5, 7)", while the fragments story makes them `["5.2","5.4","5.5","5.6","7"]`.
  - **Fix:** record the order as a `depends_on` edge between the two epics. The later epic's story must then state its pins relative to the earlier one, or this story must say which state it expects.
  - **Evidence:** `websocket-proposal-slice.md:59-65`, `websocket-ping-machine.md:86`, `websocket-fragments-machine.md:56`, `websocket-fragments-machine.md:105`, `aep plan artifact graph` (no epic-to-epic edge).
- `story:proposal-machine-layout` — its Ping/Pong test runs on a copy of `profiles/websocket` from sections 5.5.2/5.5.3. Those requirements already carry overlay statuses today, and the ping story adds a `ping` machine and `ping.protospec.yaml`.
  - **Failure mode:** once the ping story lands, the test hits the `AlreadyExists` refusals, and "`lower --check` fails only because the generated model is new" is false.
  - **Fix:** pick the epic order with a `depends_on` edge, or name a fixture machine and requirement set that does not depend on that order.
  - **Evidence:** `proposal-machine-layout.md:50-53`, `proposal-review.md:60`, `websocket-ping-machine.md:41-42`.
- `story:dns-source-catalogue` — its acceptance (`generate --check`, `task check`) reads state that `story:bulk-fetch-corpus` and `story:bulk-site-index` produce, and no edge records it.
  - **Generated pages:** the new RFC 1035 lock changes the profile column of `website/docs/catalog/rfcs/01000.md` and the overview, which are not in this story's scope.
  - **Bulk summary:** `bulk check` must find the row whose `sha256` equals the new lock.
  - **Extractor change:** a "reader or extractor fix" (`dns-source-catalogue.md:108`) changes extraction, so `EXTRACTOR_VERSION` must be raised and the summary regenerated by a networked step. That is not in scope either.
  - **Fix:** a `depends_on` edge from `epic:bulk-catalogue` to `epic:dns-profile` (or the reverse), plus the missing scope and acceptance lines in whichever story runs second.
  - **Evidence:** `dns-source-catalogue.md:83-89`, `bulk-summary-format.md:57-58`, `bulk-summary-format.md:62-68`, `bulk-site-index.md:36-45`, `bulk-site-index.md:55-58`, and the worktree `Taskfile.yml` `docs-check` step in `task check`.

What I read: 63 artifacts validated (`aep plan artifact validate` reports valid). I read all 13 drafted stories and 4 epics in full, plus `aep plan artifact relations`, `graph` (all edges, including those to `epic:websocket-profile` and the active websocket stories), and `waves`. I checked the worktree's `catalog.rs`, `rfcspec-model`, `rfcspec-doc`, `Taskfile.yml`, `profile.yaml` and `ess/domains/review.yaml`. I walked about 60 non-review edges and found no cycle. I traced about 20 acceptance reads to a producer in the set; the recorded intra-epic edges cover the in-epic ones, and none of the cross-epic reads has an edge.

Not a defect:
- **Intra-epic chains:** each one (bulk 3, ai 4, frames 3, dns 3) has a stated shared-file or produced-state reason.
- **Epic-to-epic edges:** only `epic:websocket-profile` is ever the target. No edge orders the four epics at all, which is the root of findings 2 to 4. `waves` currently places `story:proposal-record-replay` and the other no-prerequisite stories in wave 1 beside the in-flight websocket stories. I took that as intended, since the epic edge covers it.

What I could not establish:
- Which order the four epics will run in. I did not assume one; each finding names the pair, not the direction.
- Whether the dns reader fix will actually be needed. The story makes it conditional.
- Out of my lane (parallel-safety): the many file collisions `waves` prints around `AGENTS.md`, `Taskfile.yml`, `main.rs` and `Cargo.lock`.
- Out of my lane (acceptance): the `--cache`/`--out` flags of `summarise` are only specified in the second bulk story, and `requirement-from-prose` has no acceptance line in `story:proposal-record-replay`.
